Neighbourhood
Kensington has a wonderful upscale village feel and carefree charm with some amazing independent restaurants and pubs on Gloucester Road.
Super close to South Kensington, home to major museums - Natural History Museum, V+A Museum, Science Museum, leading towards Knightsbridge, with its famous shopping (Harrod’s, Harvey Nichols, and endless boutiques). Kensington High St, Notting Hill, and Portobello Road all nearby and one after another - make a day out of it!
